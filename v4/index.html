06 / GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
send the file.

Anything else on your mind?

Ask us directly ↗
Is this right for our brand?+

We work with established brands and distributors that have a substantial range. The per-SKU rates are built around catalogues of 100 or more lines; Pilot Wave S exists for smaller ranges and first product families. Below 25 lines the production line stops paying for itself, and we say so in the reply.

Can you improve existing listings?+

Yes. Dead pages, reseller pages and thin content are the usual starting point. Duplicates, suppressed products and account restrictions are reviewed before the quote, so any repair work is itemised rather than discovered later.

What information do you need from us?+

Start with your product list or catalogue link. For production we confirm the specifications, brand information and original photography or artwork, and a brand authorisation letter if we are fixing pages that already exist under your brand. Product claims must be supported by your information.

Do you need access to our Amazon account?+

Not for the sample. For filing, either a restricted user on your account or a file handover for your team. Nothing is published without your sign-off either way.

Does a person read every listing, or is it automated?+

Both, in that order. Every line passes automated checks for prohibited claims, style-guide rules and product-type schema. Then a person reads and signs off each batch before it is filed.

Who owns the content and files?+

You do. Copy, images, A+ layouts, flat-files and the keyword mapping for your range are delivered as files you keep. Your Amazon account remains yours, and everything keeps working if our work together ends.

Seller Central, Vendor Central, or hybrid?+

Seller Central first; that is where our production line, keyword base and own accounts run. For Vendor and hybrid brands the content and data files are the same, only the upload path differs. If Amazon is winding your Vendor agreement down, we set up the Seller account, Brand Registry and the migration in waves.

We own several brands. Which goes first?+

We run all of them through the same demand analysis and tell you which five to ten to back, who earns on them today instead of you, and which to leave alone. The sample covers up to five brands.

What is separate from the per-SKU price?+

Setup for a second product type, legacy repairs, physical product photography, Brand Registry or storefront recovery, and ongoing management are scoped separately and itemised in the quote. Prices exclude VAT. Advertising spend is separate from any management fee.

Do we need an ongoing contract?+

No. Start with a Pilot Wave and decide what support you need afterwards. Any ongoing service has its own agreed scope and terms, and you can stop it.
