WHAT A SHOPPER SEES

Your 2 m x 2 m liner has nine photos, on three pages. Your 4 m x 3 m has four.

The 2 m x 2 m liner is filed on three pages (B0C24PQWDD, B075C8K2VW, B082SXCL9X): each is £8.99, shows "300+ bought in past month", nine photos and the same 4,582 ratings. The sizes that follow it have fewer photos: the 3 m x 3 m, at "300+", has five, the 4 m x 3 m, at "200+", has four, and the 4 m x 4 m has four. The pumps that show a sales label have five.

Across the 50 pages we read, 38 carry fewer than seven photos and 14 carry one. One more thing a shopper can read: the 1,400LPH pump's title says 1,400 and its second bullet says "1,500 litres per hour".

So: the shorter galleries brought up to seven, best sellers first; the repeated size merged into one page with the reviews kept together; the pump's figures made to agree.

WHAT A SHOPPER CANNOT BUY

11 of the 50 pages we read show "Currently unavailable".

On 11 of the 50 pages Amazon says "Currently unavailable. We don't know when or if this item will be back in stock." Among them are the 22 m x 32 m pond liner and a 20 x 26 m liner with underlay, two koi foods, a pond air pump kit with airstones and a pressure filter O ring. 10 of the 11 carry A+ content: the pages are built, and not for sale today.

None of the 11 shows a sales label. Why a page is dark is not visible from outside, and stock and price are yours to set.

So: the weekly check lists every dark page, finds why, and files the fix that is ours to file: cases, attributes, stock notes.

WHAT IS ALREADY RIGHT

So: no change of seller, account or price; the other pages brought up to the standard of your best-covered ones.
