WHAT A SHOPPER SEES

A 50 ml cream shows £2,850 per 100 ml.

Hydra Plump Gel Cream (B0D2QBBGSJ) shows £28.50 and, beside it, "(£2,850.00 /100 ml)". The title, the jar and the page's own "Item volume" all say 50 ml, which makes it £57 per 100 ml: the line reads fifty times too high.

A smaller thing: the 3 x 9 ml set at £36.50 (B07DTF49G6) and the 2 x 9 ml set at £24.75 (B07DTG6JY3), both at "50+ bought in past month", are titled alike, "Bath & Shower Oils Gift Collection". A shopper choosing between them has the photo and the price to go on, not the title.

So: the unit count corrected so the price per 100 ml reads £57.00, and each set titled for what is in it.
