RULE 1Reviews first

Pages that already carry reviews go up first: the fudge tin's 654, the Designer Box's 483, the toffee tin's 140. A new page starts from zero; these start where they left off.

RULE 2Gift demand

"Sweets gift box", "christmas sweets" and "stocking fillers for adults" are searched every week and peak from October. Tins, drums and selections answer those searches; a single bag of mints does not.

RULE 3Shelf life and price

FBA needs 90 days of shelf life on arrival, and a £2–3 bag does not cover Amazon's fees as a single line. The 39 bags come back as multipacks in a second wave, or not at all. The inventory of all 124 pages makes that call explicit: relist, merge, or retire.
