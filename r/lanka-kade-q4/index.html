RULE 1Demand

"Toddler toys", "wooden toys", "montessori toys" and "puzzles for 3 year olds" are among the 10,000 most-typed phrases on Amazon UK, every week of the year. Number and alphabet jigsaws, playsets and arks answer them; a single £3.25 animal figure does not.

RULE 2A price that covers the fees

A single figure at £3.25 to £3.50 on your website does not cover Amazon's fees as one line, and a 1-5 jigsaw at £6.95 is on the edge. The same items as sets do: the ten-figure bags at £30, the 1-10 jigsaws at £13.50, a pair of 1-5 jigsaws. The fourteen 1-5 and four-piece pages come back as pairs or sets, or not at all.

RULE 3Size and the warehouse

A 25 mm jigsaw in its window box ships to FBA as a small standard parcel; a 60-piece playset or a jumbo floor puzzle costs more to store and to send. The first wave is the window-box range: jigsaws, block puzzles, figure bags, mini playsets. The arks, castles and furniture are priced in once FBA has quoted their size band. The inventory of all 29 pages makes the call on each: claim, merge, or retire.
