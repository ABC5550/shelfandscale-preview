WHAT A SHOPPER SEES

Three pages show a price a kilo that can't be right.

Under the price, Amazon works out what a kilo costs from the size you entered. Your Salted Nuts card, 24 bags of 50 g at £13.99, shows £279.80 a kilo, as if it held one bag; your Dry Roasted card, the same 24 bags at the same £13.99, shows £11.66. The Chilli Nuts card, 12 x 50 g at £10.99, shows £219.80. The Pulled Pork Snack, 10 x 25 g at £10.99, shows £732.67 a kilo, with "Size Name: 1.5 g (Pack of 10)" under it.

The 1 kg bags have the same kind of slip in their words: About this item on your 1kg Pork Scratchings says "100 gram", and on the 1kg Unseasoned bag "100 grams". On the 1kg Double Cooked bag, which can't be bought today, the size picker names it "100 grams".

So: the size and count set right on every page, so the price a kilo reads true; the weekly check catches the next one.

WHAT THE PAGES SAY

28 of the 40 pages carry one photo, and none has A+.

On 11 pages, About this item reads "Black Country Snacks" and "Award Winning Snacks" and nothing more. Four pages, the 1kg bags and the 12 x 60g pub card among them, list the title again, "Brand : Black Country Snacks", "Product type: MEAT", a size and a string of search words.

Your 12 x 60g pub card is the first result when someone searches "pork scratchings", and its family carries 5,240 ratings. Every page in that family has one or two photos.

So: five real lines and seven photos on each page, best sellers first: five rebuilds and new photos for four pages a month on Care S.

WHAT CANNOT BE BOUGHT

Four of the 40 pages we read can't be bought today.

Three say "Currently unavailable": the Black & Gold Big Bag 80g x 15 and the 1kg Double Cooked Crackling, both in your scratchings family with its 5,240 ratings, and the No Nonsense Double Cooked 20 x 40g. The Ghost Extreme Chilli pub card, with 97 ratings, shows no Add to basket button.

Why a page is like this is not visible from outside, and stock is yours to decide. If you would like any of them back, we can do that.

So: the weekly check lists every page that cannot be bought; the ones you want back, we bring back.
