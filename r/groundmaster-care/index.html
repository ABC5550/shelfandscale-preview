A WRONG FIELD

Six lawn seed pages say "Indoor/Outdoor usage: Indoor".

On Amazon the line sits under the title, next to Brand and Material. We read it on General Purpose Grass Seed at 1, 5, 10 and 20 kg and on Shady Lawn Grass Seed 5 kg (two pages). Your Premium Lawn Grass Seed pages that we read say Outdoor.

Amazon uses these fields in its filters and its category pages. We have not checked what a wrong value does to any one page.

So: the field set to Outdoor on all six, and every attribute on every page read again each week.

NOT ON SALE AT THE MOMENT

22 pages aren't on sale right now, rock salt among them.

On 22 of the 100 pages Amazon says "Currently unavailable. We don't know when or if this item will be back in stock." Among them are ten fertiliser pages, two White Rock Salt pages, two grass seed pages and four wildflower pages. 19 of the 22 carry ratings and A+ content.

We do not know why: stock, a line you have finished with, a plan for the season. The 10 kg size of your Premium Lawn Grass Seed shows "See All Buying Options" where Add to basket would be. If you'd like any of these back before winter, we can look into each one.

So: each page listed every week and, for the ones you want back, what holds it back looked into and the fix that is ours to file filed: cases, attributes, stock notes.

WHAT IS ALREADY RIGHT

The account and the lawn seed are in good order.

You hold the featured offer on all 76 pages that can be bought, dispatched by Gardener's Dream Ltd on 64 and by Amazon on 12. 98 of the 100 link to the GroundMaster store, 92 have A+ content, 21 have a video and 11 carry the Amazon's Choice badge. The Premium Lawn Grass Seed family has 6,832 ratings at 4.2 stars.

So the work is on the pages themselves: a field that says Indoor, titles that say what the product is, photos up to seven, A+ on the fleece pages, and the pages that are not on sale.

So: no change of seller, account or price; the other pages brought up to the standard of your lawn seed.
