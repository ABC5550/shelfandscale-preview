RULE 1Gift demand

"Gifts for him" is 224th of 1.46 million phrases and "money box" 1,135th, both typed every week of the year; "christmas gifts for him" is 637th for 27 weeks. A bottle, a kit or a box answers them. A multipack of twelve 30 g bags answers "gluten free snacks", 7,549th, instead, and waits for the second wave.

RULE 2A price that covers the fees

A £7.99 gift bottle or a £5.99 chocolate bar does not cover Amazon's fees as one line; the gift bottles as a triple pack at £22.99 and the bars as a trio at £15 do. The five Christmas Selection Box pages at £14.99 to £82 become one page with quantity options, and keep their reviews.

RULE 3Shelf life and the season

Your website prints best-before dates of 28 February 2027 or later on the bottles; FBA, which you already use on the kernels, wants 90 days on arrival. Easter, Halloween, Valentine's and Father's Day pages come back in their season, rebuilt. The inventory of all 107 decides keep, merge, retire or file for each.
