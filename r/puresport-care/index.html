WHAT A SHOPPER SEES

A box of 15 sachets shows £298.51 per 100 g.

Ultra Electrolytes Citrus Salt, 15 pack (B0D967P6B4) shows £20.00 and, beside it, "(£298.51 /100 g)". The sachet says 6.7 g and the box holds 15, which is about 100 g, so the line should read about £19.90: it reads fifteen times too high. The same box in Raspberry Salt shows £19.90 /100 g, and in the size picker the 30-pack shows £18.64.

Three more pages show the same kind of line. The 30-sachet creatine at £20.00 (B0F1NL6B9K, "800+ bought in past month") reads £400.00 /100 g: £20 for 5 g, one sachet. The Variety Pack of 30 at £33.00 (B0HL9C5GZJ) reads £550.00 /100 g: £33 for 6 g, about one sachet. The Peak Performance Bundle at £53.00 (B0F8P52GD4), 60 sachets, reads £286.49 /100 g: £53 for 18.5 g.

So: the unit count corrected on each, so the line reads £19.90 on the 15-pack and £13.33 on the creatine, as the packs say.
