03 / WHICH FIFTY, AND WHY

Gift formats first. Here is how the fifty are chosen.

Of your 74 pages, 26 are gift formats. The first wave is those 26 plus 24 more from your trade range, chosen by three rules: gift demand, a price that covers Amazon's fees, and a shelf life that clears FBA's 90-day rule.

26gift sets, selection boxes and hampers
22trade cases of 4 to 24
14single jars, sauces and catering pots
6bakery and snacks
5sweets in bags and jars
1not a food product

Green: already on Amazon and in the first fifty. The other 24 come from your trade range.

RULE 1Gift demand

"Christmas hamper", "food hamper" and "stocking fillers for adults" are among the 3,000 most-typed searches on Amazon UK and peak before Christmas. Sets, selection boxes and hampers answer them; a single jar does not.

RULE 2A price that covers the fees

A single 340 g jar at £4 to £5 does not cover Amazon's fees on its own. The same jar in a set of two or three does. Single jars come back as sets, the way the mini-jar pack already sells.

RULE 3Shelf life and format

FBA needs 90 days of shelf life on arrival. The 22 trade cases are rebuilt as retail formats or retired. The inventory of all 74 pages makes that call explicit: keep, merge, or retire.
