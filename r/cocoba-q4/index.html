RULE 1Gift demand

"Stocking fillers", "hot chocolate gift set" and "hot chocolate bombs" are among the 1,500 most-typed phrases on Amazon UK; the first two are searched every week of the year, the third 47 weeks of it. Crackers, boxes of three and spoon sets answer them; a single 45 g spoon does not.

RULE 2A price that covers the fees

A single spoon at £2.95 to £4.50 on your website does not cover Amazon's fees as one line. The same spoons as a set of three at £9.95 do. The eight single-bombe and single-spoon pages come back as sets, or not at all.

RULE 3Shelf life and the season

FBA needs 90 days of shelf life on arrival, and chocolate travels to the warehouse in the cool months. The first wave runs October to March; the four Easter eggs are the second wave. The inventory of all 28 pages makes the call on each: claim, merge, or retire.
