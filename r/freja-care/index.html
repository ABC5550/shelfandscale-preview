WHAT A SHOPPER SEES

Seven pages show a price a litre that can't be right.

Under the price, Amazon works out what a litre costs from the size entered on the page. Your Pea, Leek & Lemon and your Tomato & Red Pepper soups, four 560 ml pouches at £19.99, show £4,997.50 a litre, as if the pack held 4 ml. In the same flavour picker your Classic Chicken & Vegetable, the same four pouches, shows £8.83 a litre, which is right.

Your Broth for Cooking pages count one pouch, not the pack: six 350 ml pouches at £23.99 show £68.54 a litre on the Chicken, the Beef and the Chicken & Beef mix, and the ten-pouch packs at £38.50 show £110.00. The true figures are about £11.42 and £11.00 a litre.

So: the size and the pack count set right on all seven pages, so the price a litre reads true; the weekly check catches the next one.
