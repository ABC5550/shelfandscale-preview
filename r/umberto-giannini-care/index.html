WHAT A SHOPPER SEES

Five pages where the size, and so the price a litre, is wrong.

Under the price, Amazon works out what a litre costs from the size entered on the page. Your Curl Jelly Mask costs £9.95 and its title says 300ml, but Amazon shows £981.26 a litre, as if the jar held about 10 ml. The same figure shows beside the mask in the picker on your Scalp Scrub, Grow Scrub, Banana Coconut Detangler and Swim Proof pages. The mask's main photo is a jar marked 200 ml; your own site sells the 300 ml in a different jar.

Your Curl Custard's title says 200ml, the details under it say 300 ml, and your site sells Curly Custard as 300 ml. Amazon works from 200: £54.75 a litre, £36.50 at 300 ml. The jar in its photo reads Curl Jelly Crème. Your new Dreamy Waves Wavy Whip and Air Dry Cream read 200ml on the can and the tube, but the details say 290 ml, and Amazon prices them by the kilo, £34.31 and £37.76; at 200 ml they are £49.75 and £54.75 a litre. Your Curl Jelly Smoothie, a 225 ml bottle, shows £34.31 a kilo the same way: £44.22 a litre at 225 ml.

So: one size on each page, the same in the title, the details and the photo, so the price a litre reads true; the weekly check catches the next one.

WHAT THE PAGES SAY

22 of the 40 pages meet our standard on photos and A+.

Your Curl Jelly, with 5,185 ratings, has five photos, A+ and a video. The gaps are mostly on newer pages: 11 have no A+, among them Dreamy Waves, Curl Custard and Vanilla Gelato. Your Banana Butter, with 489 ratings, is titled "Umberto Giannini Banana Butter": no size, and nothing to say it is a leave-in conditioner. The Curl Foam's About this item is one line; the Curl Type 3 Kit has none.

A few titles carry treatment words: "Hair Loss Treatment" on the Grow Long conditioner, "Anti-Dandruff" on the Scalp Scrub. On a cosmetic each needs its evidence behind it; it is a risk to check, not a fault we are pointing at.

So: seven photos, A+ and clear words on every page, the newest first: ten rebuilds and new photos for eight pages a month on Care M.
