WHAT A SHOPPER SEES

Two pages sell on one photo, and it shows a smaller tub.

The photo is the first thing a shopper sees. Your Nutritional Yeast Flakes 1 kg page, with 200+ bought in past month and the Amazon's Choice badge, has one photo and a video: the photo shows the 250 g tub, and the size selected on the page is 1 kg. Your Guar Gum Powder 250 g page has the same shape: one photo, of the 100 g tub. Of the 39 pages that show a sales label we compared the main photo with the size in the title; these are the two that differ. It is a fact about the photos, not about what is sent.

specialingredients.co.uk shows seven photos of the 1 kg yeast: four views of the tub and three food photos. The Amazon page shows one.

So: the right tub as the first photo of each page, then the views and uses you already have on your website, inside the monthly photo quota.

A RISK, NOT AN ACCUSATION

Agar, gellan, guar, xanthan and lecithin share one variation family.

The Guar Gum Powder page lists 28 pages in its variation family, and the selector calls them "flavours": Agar Agar, Gellan Gum, Guar Gum, Gum Arabic, Methocel, Pectin, Soya and Sunflower Lecithin, Ultratex, Xanthan Gum and others. 19 of the 100 pages we read belong to it. Your yeast, by comparison, is one product in six sizes. Amazon's variation rules group sizes and colours of one product, and a family of different products can be split by Amazon, with the ratings moving with it. Today the Gellan Gum High Acyl 500 g and the Agar Agar Powder 100 g with Italian labels show the same 1,668 ratings at 4.5 stars.

Both of those pages show "Currently unavailable", as do six others of the 100: eight in all, six with A+ content. Whether a page is out of stock or held back by Amazon is not visible from outside, and the price is yours to set.

So: the weekly check lists every dark page and finds why; the family split by product, each product with reviews of its own: a case to Amazon, which decides.

WHAT IS ALREADY RIGHT

The pages are in better order than most.

You hold the featured offer on all 92 pages that can be bought, dispatched by Special Ingredients on 52 and by Amazon on 40. All 100 link to the SPECIAL INGREDIENTS store, 98 have A+ content, 89 have a video and 39 carry the Amazon's Choice badge.

So the work is narrower than on most catalogues: photos up to seven (74 of the 100 have fewer), the two pages above, the eight dark pages, and four agar agar titles that write "Vegan's & Vegetarian's".

So: no change of seller, account, price or A+; the photo gaps closed, best sellers first.
