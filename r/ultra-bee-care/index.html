WHAT A SHOPPER SEES

A swim balm filed with fish-tank water treatments.

Every Amazon page sits in an aisle, shown as a line of links above the photo. Your Pre Swim Aqua Bloc, a balm for swimmers' skin, sits in Pet Supplies › Fish & Aquatic Pets › Aquarium Accessories › Water Quality Testing & Treatment › Water Treatments. A shopper browsing skin care never meets it there. The title says "Aqua Bloc"; the tub says Aqua Block.

Your Gentle Baby Oil and Dry Scalp Balm sit in the baby aisle, but both pages set the age range to Adult; your website says the scalp balm suits all ages, especially babies. The baby oil is priced per 100 g, though the bottle holds 100 ml.

So: each page in the aisle its shoppers browse, with the age and size set right; the weekly check catches the next one.

WHAT THE PAGES SAY

Most of your pages are in order. A few slipped.

Most of your pages carry five photos or more; few reach seven. Your Aftershave Razor Bump Balm, with 315 ratings, carries one photo and one line under About this item: "Softens and conditions beard hair". Your own website shows nine photos of it. The Foot Balm carries one photo; the Dry Scalp Balm's About this item reads only "Item display volume: 50.00".

Several pages name eczema, psoriasis, scars or stretch marks in the title or bullets. In a rebuild these lines are checked against what a cosmetic may claim.

So: seven photos and five plain bullets on each page, best sellers first: five rebuilds and new photos for four pages a month on Care S.

WHAT CANNOT BE BOUGHT

Two of your pages can't be bought today.

Your Winter Balm, with 22 ratings, says "Currently unavailable", and so does a second Propolis Balm page, which shows no photo. The 100 ml Razor Bump Balm link now opens the 50 ml page; your website sells both sizes.

Why a page is off sale is not visible from outside, and your range is yours to decide. If you would like any of them back, we can do that.

So: the weekly check lists every page and size that cannot be bought; the ones you want back, we bring back.
