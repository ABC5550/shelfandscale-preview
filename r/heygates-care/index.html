WHAT A SHOPPER SEES

A 20 kg sack priced by the kilo as if it were one kilo.

Under the price, Amazon works out what a kilo costs from the size entered on the page. Your Country Layers Meal is a 20 kg sack at £19.30; the bag in the photo says 20kg net, but the page is entered as 1 kg, so Amazon shows £19.30 a kilo. Your Mixed Poultry Corn, a 20 kg sack at the same £19.30 from the same shop, shows £0.97. The same page lists the meal's item form as "Pellet"; the bag calls it a coarsely ground ration.

Your Layers Pellets + Flubenvet 5 kg bag has two pages: one with 1,472 ratings, the other with 424, both showing the same 5kg Net bag. The second is titled "5 or 20 kg (5kg)". Merged, one page carries all the ratings.

So: the size set right on every page, so the price a kilo reads true; the two Flubenvet pages merged into one; the weekly check catches the next one.
