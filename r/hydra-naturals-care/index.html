WHAT A SHOPPER SEES

A one-litre bottle priced by the litre as if it held 100 ml.

Under the price, Amazon works out what a litre costs from the size entered on the page. Your one-litre Multicohol costs £12.79; the bottle reads 1 LITRE and the title says 1000ml, but the size list says 100 ml, so Amazon shows £127.90 a litre. The 500 ml bottle beside it, at £11.99, shows £23.98. Your 50 ml makeup brush cleaner spray at £3.99 shows £39.90 a litre, priced as if it held 100 ml: £79.80 in truth.

Five of your grapeseed oil pages, from 10 ml to 500 ml, are titled "Ultra Naturals", while the bottle in each photo reads Hydra Naturals. The 500 ml page also lists almond, avocado, castor, coconut and jojoba oil as its active ingredients, under a title that says 100% pure grapeseed oil.

So: the size set right on every page, so the price a litre reads true; each title named as its bottle is; the weekly check catches the next one.

WHAT THE PAGES SAY

24 of the 39 pages carry three photos or fewer.

Your Nature's Moisture Aloe Vera Gel 1kg, with 1,259 ratings, is the 7th result when someone searches "aloe vera gel". Its two photos are the same picture, 679 pixels wide, under the 1,000 Amazon needs to let a shopper zoom. The 500 ml tub, result 6, is the same.

Two 50 ml Sweet Almond Oil pages share one title, word for word: one is the amber glass bottle with a dropper and seven photos, the other a plastic bottle with one photo. From the titles, a shopper can't tell which bottle they will get.

So: seven photos and a clear title on each page, best sellers first: five rebuilds and new photos for four pages a month on Care S.

WHAT CANNOT BE BOUGHT

Four of your sizes can't be bought today.

Your Tea Tree Essential Oil 20ml says "Currently unavailable". Three older links now open a different size: Jojoba Oil 30 ml, with 308 ratings, opens the 50 ml; Multicohol 30 ml opens the one-litre bottle; a 100 ml makeup brush cleaner opens a 50 ml one.

Why a size is off sale is not visible from outside, and your range is yours to decide. If you would like any of them back, we can do that.

So: the weekly check lists every page and size that cannot be bought; the ones you want back, we bring back.
