WHAT A SHOPPER SEES

A box of 24 flapjacks priced as if it were one bar.

Under the price, Amazon works out a price a kilo or a bar from what is entered on the page. Your Chocoholic, Fruit and Raspberry boxes are entered as 90 g, so a box of 24 bars at £24.99 shows £277.67 a kilo; 24 bars are well over 2 kg, about £11 to £12 a kilo. The Chocoholic two-box option shows the same £277.67.

Your Original, Cherry Bakewell and Peanut Butter boxes show "£24.99 / count": the box is counted as one, so a bar reads £24.99, not £1.04. Your Oat Flour 12kg Box page is named "3kg Pack" in its own size picker.

So: the size and count set right on all seven flapjack pages, so the price a kilo or a bar reads true; the weekly check catches the next one.

WHAT THE PAGES SAY

Your oat pages are in good order. The flapjack pages are not, yet.

Your Oat Flour, Gluten Free Oat Flour and Oat Bran pages carry nine photos, a video and A+. The flapjack boxes carry four photos; the new Fruit and Raspberry boxes two, no A+, and one line in About this item. On the Chocoholic page that line is "Delicious Chocoholic Oat Flapjacks". Cherry Bakewell and Peanut Butter are filed under Breakfast Cereal › Oatmeal, not with your other flapjacks.

The line under your Original's title says "High fibre, high protein". The label on your website gives 4.4 g fibre and 5.8 g protein per 100 g, below what UK rules ask for those words: a risk worth a look.

So: seven photos and five real lines on every flapjack page, the claims checked against your label: five rebuilds and new photos for four pages a month on Care S.

WHAT CANNOT BE BOUGHT

10 of the 22 pages we read can't be bought today.

All ten say "Currently unavailable": Oat Flour 3kg and the 4 x 3kg box, Porridge Oats, Medium Oatmeal and Jumbo Oats 1kg, Whole Californian Almonds 400g, Vital Wheat Gluten 130g, and three Gluten Free Oat Flour pages (200g, 2 x 200g, 12kg). Most carry nine photos and a video: the pages are built, and not for sale today.

Why a page is like this is not visible from outside, and stock is yours to decide. If you would like any of them back, we can do that.

So: the weekly check lists every page that cannot be bought; the ones you want back, we bring back.
