RULE 1Ratings first

Pages that already carry ratings go up first: 23 unavailable pages have 100 or more, 938 on the Salt & Caramel 295 g jar, 810 on Classic Caramel, 704 on the 125 g jar. A new page starts from zero; these start where they left off.

RULE 2One page per jar

Where a 2019 page and a 2025 page carry the same jar at the old and the new weight, the one with the ratings stays and the other is merged or retired. The inventory of all 95 pages makes that call explicit, page by page, before anything is signed.

RULE 3Price and shelf life

A £3.25 stick does not cover Amazon's fees as a single line; a jar from £10.50 does. Amazon's warehouse needs 90 days of shelf life on arrival, so the launch stock is planned from your batch dates.
